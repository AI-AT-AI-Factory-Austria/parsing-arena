Q3 Operations Report

Warehouse throughput, delivery performance and outlook

1. Executive summary

Total shipments grew by 12.4% quarter over quarter, driven by the new Leipzig hub that opened in July. On-time delivery improved to 96.1%, while the cost per parcel fell from €3.42 to €3.18. Returns processing remains the main bottleneck and is addressed in Section 3.

Scope: all four German hubs, excluding the pilot site in Graz.

3. Key actions for Q4

  • Automate returns sorting in Hamburg (target: −30% handling time).
  • Extend Leipzig night shifts to absorb seasonal peak volume.
  • Renegotiate last-mile contracts in Munich before 1 December.

Capacity planning uses the utilisation ratio

U = λ / (c · μ)

where λ is the arrival rate of parcels per hour, c the number of active sorting lines and μ the service rate per line.

Figures are preliminary and unaudited. Leipzig opened on 3 July 2026, so no Q2 values exist.

5. Outlook

For Q4 we expect volume to rise by another 18–22% because of the seasonal peak. Hiring for 140 temporary positions started in September. The board approved a budget of €2.4 million for the returns automation project, with go-live planned for February 2027.

Risks: fuel prices, driver availability and a possible rail strike in November.
